---
title: Members and invites
description: Add people to an organization with copyable invite links and role-based access.
---

An invite is a copyable link. Trema sends no email, so no SMTP server is required—share the link over any channel you already use.

## Roles

Every member holds one role on the organization, and the role decides what they can do:

| Role | Allows |
| --- | --- |
| `viewer` | Read. |
| `member` | Read, create and edit items, install skills. |
| `admin` | Everything a member can, plus managing connectors, policies, and members. |
| `owner` | Everything an `admin` can, plus organization administration. |

The organization's agent principal holds no role; roles belong to people.

## Invites

Members with the `admin` or `owner` role create invites from the member settings page or through the invites endpoint in the [API reference](/api). An invite carries:

- the role it grants
- the scope it applies to, defaulting to the organization
- an expiry, defaulting to seven days after creation

The response contains the join link exactly once. Only a hash of the link's token is stored, so a lost link cannot be recovered—create a new invite instead.

Pending invites appear on the member settings page. Revoke an invite when its link should stop working. A revoked invite cannot be previewed or redeemed.

## Redeeming

The recipient opens the link, signs in or creates an account, and sees the organization's name and who invited them. Confirming joins them with the invite's role. An invite is single-use: once redeemed, or past its expiry, the link is rejected.

On a dedicated deployment the link is also what makes the account possible: sign-ups without a pending invite are refused, unless the operator sets [`TREMA_OPEN_SIGNUP=true`](/configuration#account-creation). A hosted deployment leaves sign-up open.

## Changing roles

Roles change through the members endpoints in the [API reference](/api), and a change takes effect immediately. One rule holds: the organization's last owner cannot be demoted.

## Deactivating members

Owners and admins can deactivate a member from the member settings page or through the principals endpoint. Deactivation keeps the principal and grants, but blocks sign-in, revokes service credentials, and removes identity links. Items and other records the member created stay intact.

The organization's last active owner cannot be deactivated. An invite cannot reactivate a deactivated member.

## Reactivating members

Reactivation restores access for the principal and keeps the existing grants. It does not restore revoked credentials or removed identity links. The member must connect those again through the normal flows.

## SMTP

Nothing in the current release sends email. SMTP support—emailed invites and password resets—is planned as optional configuration; the copyable link stays the baseline.
